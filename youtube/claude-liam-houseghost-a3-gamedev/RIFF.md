# RIFF — the film's stance

The film is a case study, not a checklist: three ordinary objects, and the path each took from a
brief to a scene. Its argument is the hesitant writer's correction — the numbers say whether the
props *arrived* correctly; only looking says whether they *read* correctly. Every riff is on
inspected output: the check's own table, my own screenshots, the engine's own frames. Problems
appear as caught-and-fixed moments, and the one unresolved one — the music box from behind —
is stated plainly as the next step.
